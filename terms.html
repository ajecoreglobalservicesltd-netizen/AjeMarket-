How AjeMarket works

  1. Find a listing.
  2. Contact the seller.
  3. Meet safely and inspect/verify the item.
  4. Agree on the transaction.
  5. Pay the seller directly only after you are satisfied.

Payment

AjeMarket is a marketplace, not a wallet, bank or escrow service. AjeMarket does not hold the buyer's purchase money for normal marketplace transactions.

Scam warning

Do not pay deposits, “transport fees”, “clearance fees” or full purchase prices before seeing and verifying the product and seller. Report suspicious listings.

Platform responsibility

AjeMarket provides listings and communication tools. Users remain responsible for verifying sellers, products, ownership, authenticity, price and transaction details before paying.
